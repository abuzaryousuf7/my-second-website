About me

Meet the human behind the code.

I make websites that look sharp, load fast and are simple to use.

My name is Abuzar. I got hooked on the web the first time I changed a page's colour and saw it update on my own screen. That small moment turned into a daily habit of building, breaking and fixing things.

I taught myself HTML and CSS first, because strong basics matter more than any framework. Then I added JavaScript, React and design tools. Today I build full websites for people who want something clean, quick and made with care.

I like working directly with the person who owns the idea. I ask plenty of questions at the start, show progress often and never leave a project half finished.

How I work

Four rules. Hover one.

01

Start simple

The best page does its job with the least. I add things only when they help the visitor.

02

Speed is a feature

People leave slow sites. I compress, trim and test until pages open almost instantly, even on a weak connection.

03

Build for everyone

Good contrast, keyboard access and clear text. A site should work for every visitor on every device.

04

Explain everything

I write clear code and tell you what I did and why, so you are never stuck depending on me.

Price depends on the number of pages and features. After a short chat I send a fixed quote, so there are no surprises later.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every site I build is designed for phones first and tested on small, medium and large screens.

Can you update my site later?

Of course. I offer simple monthly support for changes, fixes and speed checks, or I can teach you to make small edits yourself.

Do you work with clients abroad?

Yes. I work with clients in any time zone and keep in touch by email or video call.
